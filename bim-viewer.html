IFC in the browser

No installation. No licence.

Drag in an IFC file and it opens — IFC 2x3 and IFC 4, from Revit, ArchiCAD and the rest. Real project models of a hundred megabytes and more, parsed with a streaming reader built for construction-site laptops and tablets.

Privacy by design

The file never leaves your machine.

The free viewer parses the model locally in your browser — nothing is uploaded, stored or logged. Open a confidential tender model without sending it anywhere. That is not a promise in a policy; it is how the viewer is built.

Measuring tools

Measure lengths and areas.

Double-click two points for a length, corner by corner for an area. Cut sections through the model, isolate load-bearing structure, and click any element for its properties.

Area analysis

Floor areas out of the model.

Gross and net floor areas, saleable area and per-storey breakdowns — read from the model's own surfaces and slabs, with an honest plausibility check that flags scale errors and mixed layers instead of guessing.

Residential units

Counts units — even without spaces.

Many architect exports carry no room objects at all. The viewer measures unit signals — entrance doors in party walls, confirmed by kitchens and furniture — and shows every competing signal so a wrong reading is visible, never hidden.

Model library

Save models when you need them.

Viewing is free, always. If you want to keep models in a library and open them from any machine, storage is 199 kr per month — activated inside the viewer, invoiced by email, cancel any time.
