The Builtly toolbox

Every tool. Five toolboxes. One project.

Each tool is deep professional software in its own discipline — and they all read the same model of the project. Organised the way the work is organised: map & analysis, development, economics, sales and administration.

01Map & analysisBefore you buy

Property data, planning status and feasibility — the decision basis before the project exists.

  • Property mapProperty data, zoning and ground conditions on the map.Live
  • Feasibility studyFrom site data to volume study — generated all the way.Live
  • Climate risk screeningFlood, sea level, wind and landslide — EU Taxonomy screening.Live
  • Technical due diligenceStructured technical assessment for transactions.Live
02DevelopmentThe engineering disciplines

The discipline deliverables of the design phase — produced by the platform, controlled and signed by responsible engineers.

  • Geotechnical reportsGround conditions, FEM analysis and 3D ground models.Live
  • Structural drawings & basis (RIB)Load-bearing systems, quantities and structural basis.Live
  • Fire conceptFire cells, escape routes and door classes against TEK17 — drawn as deliverable sheets.Live
  • Orientation planEmergency-services orientation plans.Part of Fire concept
  • Acoustic reportNoise maps and facade requirements.Live
  • Traffic analysisTrip generation, parking and access logic.Live
  • HSE plan (SHA)Safety, health and working environment for the site.Live
  • BREEAM pre-assessmentEarly BREEAM-NOR potential, credits and material strategy.Live
  • Environmental follow-up planWaste, reuse, emissions and nature on site.Live
  • Contaminated ground action planHandling of contaminated ground.Part of Geo / Environment
03EconomicsFrom quantities to yield

The project in numbers — quantities, tenders, value and financing control on the same model.

  • Tender documentsTender discovery and assessment, structured tender packages.Live
  • Quantities & scopeQuantities, areas and revisions with traceability to source.Live
  • Area & yield optimisationBIM to cash flow — area, energy grades, Monte Carlo and IRR.Live
  • ValuationValue assessment in kroner, phased cash flow.Part of Area & yield
  • Construction loan controlDraw-down control against actual progress.Live
  • Credit basisStructured decision basis for lenders.Live
04Sales & marketingSteered against time

Prospects, project sites and campaigns — with sales pace measured against the target, not gut feeling.

  • Sales consoleNewsletters, SMS, prospect lists, sales targets, pace and Friday reports.Live
  • Project websiteLanding pages on the project's own domain with interest registration.Part of Sales console
  • ProspectusBrowsable prospectus (flipbook) with shareable links.Part of Sales console
  • New-build radarMarket monitoring of competing new-build projects.Planned
