§ 01The workdayBuilt for the first fifteen minutes of the morning

Every project, one glance.

01Morning overview

Which project needs you today?

Every project on one line: status, phase, the latest file and the next deadline — with the projects that need attention on top. A red row always carries its reason: an overdue delivery, a deadline this week, or thirty silent days.

02My page

What is on you — across everything.

All open deadlines that concern you across every project, nearest first, with countdown. On fifteen projects, "what falls due this week" is one page — not fifteen tabs.

03The inbox

Everything that happened, measured.

Incoming bids, new tender questions, transmittal receipts and the disciplines' BIM uploads — read straight from the project registers, so the inbox is always in step with reality. One click lands you on the right section.

§ 02DocumentsRevision control that cannot be talked around

Every upload passes the gate.

  • 01
    The naming standard is applied for you
    Discipline codes read from the folder's own catalogue entry, sequence numbers assigned per project — and a doorman that recognises when a file's content belongs to another discipline and suggests the right folder. You can always override.
  • 02
    A new file becomes a new revision — never a copy
    Same document number means same document: the full revision history lives on the document, and "where is the current fire concept?" always has exactly one answer.
  • 03
    One folder standard — and your own
    Every project is born with the discipline-based standard structure, so every project answers the same way. Prefer your own structure? Build the company template folder by folder — or upload a screenshot of the old project hotel and have it interpreted into a proposal you approve.
  • 04
    Moving in is one operation, not a week
    Drop the whole archive as a zip. Every file is classified by prioritised signals — naming standard, the folder it came from, conventions, then AI — with the source shown on every row, and nothing is uploaded before you have approved the review. A file without a trustworthy signal ends honestly in the "choose folder" pile.
  • 05
    The formal transmittal is the archive trail
    A transmittal freezes which documents, in which revisions, to whom, for what purpose — and can never be edited afterwards. Every recipient gets a personal link, downloads are verified against the fingerprint from the moment of sending, and the receipt has three honest levels: opened, downloaded, receipt confirmed.
§ 03BIMNo software to install — same viewer on every device

BIM straight in the browser.

01Federated model

The common model updates itself.

There is no assembly step: the coordination model is always the sum of every discipline's IFC in its current revision. When HVAC uploads a new revision, it is in the common model the same second — and the project lead is notified automatically.

02X-ray vision

See through the wall. Stand in the room.

Make the architect's model transparent and see the pipes, ducts and cables behind the wall. "Stand here" puts you at eye height where you clicked, the ceiling slides away — and Esc always returns exactly the camera you had.

03AR on site

The model in the room in front of you.

Every current IFC revision is automatically built for the phone's own AR view — placed in the room at true scale, nothing to install. What you see in AR is the same revision that lives in the project.

04Area analysis

Honest numbers, measured from the model.

Gross/net, BRA and sellable area with everything held outside housing shown as its own lines — and a sanity check that reads the model's own numbers and says when something cannot be right. What cannot be measured is shown as a gap, never a guess.

05Units without room data

Counts homes even when the model has no rooms.

When the architect's export carries no IfcSpace at all, units are counted from independent signals in the model — doors, kitchens, groups — with a confidence badge and the competing signals side by side. Your own figure from the prospectus always wins.

06IFC check

Quality-check an export before it is shared.

Run the exact same reading on any IFC from your own machine — area analysis, unit signal, confidence marks — without uploading anything: the model never leaves your computer. And once a model is read, the next opening of the same revision takes seconds.

§ 04Ask Builtly AIA project is thousands of pages

Ask the project. Get the source.

01Always with a source

Every answer cites its documents.

Ask in your own words — "what is the sound class requirement between apartments?" — and the answer is read out of the project's own documents, with title, folder and revision. If the answer is not in the documents, it says so. It never guesses, and it never invents a number.

02Straight to the page

Read the sentence yourself.

The citation opens the PDF on the very page the answer was read from. An answer you cannot verify is not an answer.

03Revision-aware

A right answer from rev. B is a wrong answer in rev. C.

If a source document has received a new revision after the answer was given, the answer says so. And the assistant reads exactly the documents you have access to — a guest with one folder gets answers from that folder, never from the rest of the project.

04Status report draft

The numbers are measured. The words are yours.

Ask for a status report draft: the figures are measured from the project's own registers — documents, revisions, deviations, deadlines — the text is edited by a human, and the result is saved as a marked AI draft through the ordinary revision gate.

The rule

The same rule everywhere AI is used in the Prosjekthotell: AI suggests — a human approves — the engine executes. There is no path from an AI suggestion to a registered fact without a person saying yes.

§ 05DeadlinesDeadlines that do the nagging for you

Nothing slips silently.

  • 01
    Delivery deadlines with a built-in reminder chain
    A deadline is set on a discipline role with what is to be delivered, a date and the delivery folder. The system takes the nagging: a friendly reminder three days ahead, e-mail and SMS on the due date, and a follow-up every Monday morning until the delivery is reported — measured against who is actually on the project at each send.
  • 02
    Two separated responsibilities
    The discipline delivers and reports delivered; the one who set the deadline reviews and closes. Nobody closes their own delivery — and a rejection requires a written reason, so the discipline knows exactly what is missing. The reviewer is reminded too.
  • 03
    The Monday report — always
    Every Monday morning, one e-mail per project to everyone involved: what happened last week, who did it, what was delivered, and what is coming — overdue deadlines first and in the subject line. Even a quiet week is a report, because a report that never came cannot be told apart from one that broke.
  • 04
    Nothing is stamped "sent" unless it went
    The notifications that actually went out sit on the deadline row, with time. A failed send is retried at the next run — never a silently skipped reminder.
§ 06The tender roomEqual treatment as a system property

The whole tender process, one room.

  • 01
    Build the basis from the folders you already have
    Pick "20 RIB", "10 ARK" or whole branches — everything counts in its current approved revision, and documents without an approved revision are held out by name. A hole in a tender basis is an informed choice, never a silence.
  • 02
    Locked at first share
    The moment the basis is shared with the first bidder, the room locks: basket and terms can no longer be changed — not from the interface, not through the API, not by an approved AI suggestion. The lock is enforced in the engine itself. Everyone competes on the same basis.
  • 03
    Anonymous Q&A by design
    Bidders ask from their side — who asked is not stored at all. The answer is published to every bidder at the same time, so everyone prices the same information.
  • 04
    Bids on personal links, stamped honestly
    Bids are delivered on the bidder's personal link and registered with a timestamp. A late delivery is stamped late — never rejected, never hidden. No bidder ever sees another's files.
  • 05
    AI compilation that never invents a number
    When the bids are in, the AI reads and compiles: delivery per bidder, amounts quoted only with their source file, deviations and reservations to clarify. Decision support — it never picks a winner.
  • 06
    A bidder page that needs no account
    The personal link opens a work page with everything in four steps: confirm receipt, the documents grouped by discipline, Q&A, and delivery. BIM models open straight in the browser — in the revision that was sent. For smaller trades, the light request gives the same basket without the full apparatus.
§ 07Site & operationsFrom the gate to facility management

Out on the site — and into operations.

01§ 15 check-in

A QR code in the gate. A statutory list out.

Everyone checks in on their phone — no account, no app. The electronic register under the Norwegian client regulation § 15 is kept and checked daily, with a CSV export ready for the client, safety delegate and the Labour Inspection. Missing details never stop anyone in the gate — they are flagged for correction instead.

02Site diary

Crewing measured in the gate.

Crew per discipline proposed from the actual check-ins, weather, work and incidents — kept per day, next to the check-in list. Where the foreman's report differs from the gate, the gap is stated in plain text.

03Checklists & deviations

Works in the basement. Syncs when you are out.

Safety rounds and inspections filled in as you walk, with a photo per item. Field deviations pinned on the drawing or straight in the BIM model — the pin freezes the revision it was set in and never moves by itself. Everything works without coverage and syncs when the net is back, exactly once.

04FDV — into operations

The register that takes the building into its life.

Components read out of the FDV documents by AI and approved one by one; maintenance deadlines derived from the last performed check plus the interval — never stored as dates that can drift. Every interval names its source, and expiring warranties become visible while you can still claim them.

§ 08SecurityThe properties everything else rests on

Built to be audited.

01Separated companies

Every company's data is keyed on its organisation number in the storage itself — two companies with the same project code can never see each other's projects.

02Everything is logged

Uploads, downloads, moves, shares, phase changes and receipts are written to the activity log — on the project and on the individual document.

03Fail-closed

If an access is unclear, the answer is no. A withdrawn share goes dark immediately, expired links close themselves, and "does not exist" and "not yours" give the same answer — nobody can map the project by probing.

04Honesty as a principle

Numbers are shown with their source, AI suggests and a human approves, and what could not be done is reported — never a silent success that did not happen.
